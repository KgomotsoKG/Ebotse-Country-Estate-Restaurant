Legacy of Excellence

A Restaurant Like No Other

Established within the prestigious Ebotse Golf & Country Estate, our restaurant is a sanctuary for the senses. We bring together the tranquility of the Gauteng Highveld and the sophistication of modern South African cuisine.

Whether you are enjoying a post-game lunch overlooking the fairways or a candlelit dinner on our expansive terrace, every moment at Ebotse is curated to feel both grand and intimately personal. Our kitchen celebrates the bounty of the season, working closely with local artisans to ensure every plate tells a story of quality and craft.

From milestone celebrations to casual estate living, we invite you to experience hospitality that feels like home, elevated to the extraordinary.
